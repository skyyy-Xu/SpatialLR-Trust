- Leave-one-biological-sample-out high tier: 218 to 217; 48 tier changes.

## Semi-simulation

On the 510-row common-callable subset, SpatialLR-Trust had AUROC 0.621 and average precision 0.824. Results were mixed across method-native scopes and perturbations. These point estimates do not establish statistical superiority, calibrated probabilities or experimental truth.
